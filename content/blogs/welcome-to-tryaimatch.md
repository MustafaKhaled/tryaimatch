# Welcome to tryaimatch

The AI tool landscape doubles every quarter. New tools launch every week, old ones rebrand or pivot, and every category has 30+ "best X" listicles competing for clicks. Picking a tool by reading reviews has become a full-time job — and most of those reviews are sponsored anyway.

So we built something different.

## How it works

You answer a few short questions about how you actually work. Not "what's your favourite colour" filler — real signal: the format you produce, the constraints you have, the budget you're working with.

Then we score every tool in the category against your answers, applying hard filters (no point recommending a paid-only tool if you said "free only") and weighted preferences (someone who needs cross-file context gets different recommendations than someone debugging one-liners).

The result: a ranked top 3, with the reasons each one matched.
